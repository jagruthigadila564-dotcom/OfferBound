# **OfferBound**

AI-Powered Resume Analyzer \& Personalized Mock Interview Platform

## Project Overview



OfferBound AI is a full-stack web application designed to help job seekers improve their chances of getting hired by providing AI-driven resume analysis and personalized interview preparation.



The application allows users to upload their resume and provide the job description of the role they wish to apply for. The system analyzes the resume against the job description using AI and ATS (Applicant Tracking System) principles to determine how well the candidate matches the job requirements.



The platform identifies matched skills, missing skills, strengths, weaknesses, and provides an ATS compatibility score along with detailed suggestions to improve the resume.



After the resume analysis, users can start an AI-powered mock interview. The interview is dynamically generated based on the uploaded resume, target job description, projects, experience, and missing skills rather than using a fixed set of questions.



The AI interviewer asks technical, behavioral, project-based, and HR questions in a conversational manner. Based on the user's responses, the AI evaluates technical knowledge, communication, clarity, problem-solving ability, and overall interview performance. The interview can adapt its difficulty by asking follow-up questions according to the user's answers.



At the end of the interview, the platform generates a detailed performance report containing interview scores, strengths, weaknesses, personalized improvement suggestions, and recommended topics to study before attending the actual interview.



The primary goal of CareerPilot AI is to bridge the gap between job applications and interview preparation by providing candidates with a single platform to analyze their resumes, identify skill gaps, improve their profiles, and practice realistic AI-driven interviews before applying for jobs.



###### **Core Features**



* User Registration and Login
* Resume Upload (PDF/DOCX)
* Job Description Input
* AI Resume Parsing
* Resume vs Job Description Matching
* ATS Compatibility Score
* Skill Gap Analysis
* Resume Improvement Suggestions
* Personalized Learning Recommendations
* AI-Generated Mock Interview
* Adaptive Interview Question Generation
* Technical, HR, Behavioral, and Project-Based Questions
* AI-Based Interview Evaluation
* Detailed Interview Feedback
* Downloadable Performance Report
* Interview History Dashboard
